# The box

`ultima3-box-c64.jpg`: the front of Ultima III's Commodore 64 box (716 ×
1006), Denis Loubet's 1983 painting for Origin Systems under the box's
EXODUS / ULTIMA III lettering. It is the source of the desktop app's
Steam artwork: `node tools/steam-art.mjs` in `web/` makes
`desktop/build/steam/` from it (see that script for each picture). Of the
scans to hand it has the cleanest painting: the larger FM-77 box's has a
sticker and its Japanese title over the foot, and the Atari's and a second
C64 scan carry text over the lower half. Its one blemish, the
"Commodore 64 disc" label at the bottom right, the script covers with the
lava of the left side.

Where the title stands alone (the logo, the wide capsule) it is not this
scan's but drawn afresh after it, by `tools/title-art.mjs`.

The painting remains its owners'; it is altered only by that cover, the
crops and the hero's cut (the demon and his wings cut from the cavern).
