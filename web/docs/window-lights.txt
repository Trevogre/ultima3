Night windows (Depth / sprite cards)

Van Gogh town and castle cards have reviewed warm window regions. Only dark,
blue window pixels inside those regions gain emission; architecture, flags and
classic tiles retain their source pixels. Both animated building cutouts remove
the reviewed background gaps below their flags without changing RGB artwork.

With day/night lighting enabled, known settlement cards spill warm light over
nearby ground, fading within 3.5 tiles. The strength follows the existing cosmetic
daylight colour, rather than a second clock. Full overhead and model scenes omit
these sprite-card lights. Disabling day/night removes both glow and spill.

Light pools use the visibility-filtered landscape. Unknown cells block light and
never receive it. Raised mountains/walls/counters block rays from a 0.65-tile-high
window, giving ground shadows. Vegetation uses a small soft blocking footprint;
this is an approximation, not a projected per-leaf silhouette. Pools do not
illuminate recessed water, cube faces, or actor cards. Cubes still depth-occlude
the ground light normally. No gameplay, discovery or movement effects.

Pool textures are built at 64x64 per settlement and reused until scenery, raised
terrain options or party-relative layout changes. Night strength is a material
opacity update. All textures/materials are disposed on rebuild and scene disposal.
