Van Gogh lava effects

Settings / Pause > Visuals > 3D Settings > Lava (2).
Cycles Rising, Surface, Off. Rising is the new default; saved Off stays Off.
The individual mode is remembered. Classic, model scenes and other tile sets
retain their existing presentation.

Rising retains the existing animated ground tile. Two staggered sources per known
lava tile grow solid painted domes above the surface and eject three droplets per
burst. Droplets follow rising/falling arcs, shrinking before returning to the
surface. Sphere meshes have real depth and use the selected lava painting with
warm highlights and curved brush-stroke modulation. They are neither cards nor
flat ground highlights. Geometry is instanced; animation runs on the GPU.

Surface retains the earlier procedural impasto circles/swirl shader over the
painted floor, as an independent selectable presentation. No raster art changes.
Both modes use stable world-coordinate seeds and the existing animation clock;
paused or hidden maps do not update the displayed animation. Known scenery alone
supplies lava positions. Depth testing allows nearer cubes and actors to occlude
the effect. It never changes damage, movement, discovery or other gameplay.

Review without saves/preferences: /tools/preview-lava.html. Select Rising,
Surface or Off, pause animation, and rotate around the occluding cube.
Tests cover preference migration, saved Off/Surface, library selection, known
lava placement, volumetric mesh instancing and depth testing.
