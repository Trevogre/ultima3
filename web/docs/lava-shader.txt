Van Gogh lava bubbles

Settings / Pause > Visuals > 3D Settings > Lava bubbles (2).
Enabled by default in Depth. The individual choice is saved through the existing
3D preferences; Off becomes Custom. Classic, model scenes and other tile sets
retain their existing presentation.

LavaSurface samples the current Van Gogh lava animation frame, then overlays
three staggered procedural bubbles per known tile: warm shaded domes, broken
golden rims and curved impasto-like strokes. No raster assets are modified.
A stable world-tile hash varies location and phase. Growth and fading take about
four to five seconds; this is cosmetic and never changes lava damage or movement.

The batch uses one instanced ground plane per visibility-filtered lava tile,
placed 0.002 above the original terrain. Depth testing preserves foreground
occlusion. Highlights run through the existing map animation invalidation loop
and stop updating while the game map is paused or hidden. Existing zoom filtering,
daylight tint and inversion apply. GPU resources are disposed with the map scene.

To review without saves/preferences: /tools/preview-lava.html. Toggle bubbles,
pause the animation, and rotate around the occluding cube.
Tests cover saved Off migration, Custom detection, style/tile-set restrictions,
known lava placement, and ground depth testing.
