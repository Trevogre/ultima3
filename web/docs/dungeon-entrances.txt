Mountain-face dungeon entrances

In angled tile-card mode (Buildings + Mountains enabled) and model mode,
a dungeon entrance is painted on one fixed, upright side of an adjacent
mountain cube. Its original entry square becomes grass in the renderer.
The map data, movement, E command, discovery and original atlas are unchanged.
Overhead tile-card mode keeps the original tile. Disabling Buildings or
Mountains restores the existing entrance presentation.

Edit DUNGEON_FACE_OVERRIDES in src/ui/dungeonEntrances.ts. Keys are map/X/Y;
the direction points FROM the square where E is pressed TO the host mountain.
For example, north means the mountain immediately north, with its SOUTH face
looking back at the entry square. Coordinates are zero-based map coordinates.

The dungeon at 49,34 has fire terrain, not mountains, around it. It keeps its
existing entrance. No mountain is invented or substituted for fire.

Inference considers cardinal mountain neighbors, strongly favors a walkable
square behind the entry on the same axis, then a further walkable square,
then a mountain behind the host. Ties use north/east/south/west order. The
six real-map choices are pinned so exploration or zoom cannot flip a face.
Only already-visible/discovered entry and host terrain can render the facade.
Unknown hosts are never exposed, and a missing authored host does not silently
select another side. Local-only and expanded scenery use the same table.

A host remains a full cube with a vertical face even when stepped mountains
are enabled. Model sets give that host a solid rock cliff instead of a peak.
The selected sprite set's complete dungeon tile is used as a surface decal;
it never billboards, and depth testing hides it from behind the mountain.
No new artwork, colour-key removal or reconstructed pixels are required.
