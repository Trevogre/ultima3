Vertical force fields

Angled sprite views and model scenes display a tile-wide, one-tile-high barrier
with 0.08-tile thickness. Front, back, top and edges use the selected set's existing
animated force-field artwork. UVs keep the broad faces upright. Neighbouring walls,
counters, doors and fields determine orientation, independent of camera movement.

The barrier uses only visibility-filtered cells and writes/tests depth, so nearer
walls and figures occlude it normally. Its floor uses the underlying floor artwork;
there is no duplicate field painted on the ground. Classic and full-overhead sprite
views retain their original tiles. Model scenes use the barrier instead of the old
portal-shaped model. Gameplay, movement, visibility and original art are unchanged.

Review without saves/preferences: /tools/preview-force-fields.html.
Tests: forceFieldWalls.test.ts (geometry, orientation, frame refresh, hidden cells).
