Town visibility: Classic / Sightlines

Settings or Pause > Visuals > Town visibility toggles the mode immediately.
Classic is the default and retains the original 11x11 visibility algorithm.
The choice is remembered in display preferences, independently of Classic,
Depth, or model presentation presets. No game-save migration is needed.

Sightlines traces grid rays across the current town or castle. Open squares
use centre-to-centre rays; opaque tiles also test their exposed near faces so
a continuous hall wall remains visible at a grazing angle. Opaque wall/door/mountain/forest surfaces are visible, but hide what
is behind them. Diagonal rays touching a wall edge are blocked, preventing
corner peeking. NPCs do not block sight; their underlying terrain does.
Low counters and letter barriers block movement but allow vision over them.
The original entry/movement, combat, AI, targeting and lighting rules remain.
Overworld, Ambrosia, combat and first-person dungeon visibility are unchanged.

There is no artificial 11x11 cutoff for current town sight in 3D. The local
composition stays 11x11 for existing effects and classic drawing, while the
optional full-town filtered view feeds the expanded scenery and distant NPCs
in card and model modes. Native cards use their existing art, ground heights,
depth tests and adjacent-wall clearance. A visible distant figure disappears
as soon as a wall or closed door blocks it. No raw-map actor list is rendered.

The automap records only sight-admitted squares. Exploration memory does not
expose town rooms in the game view: walking away or closing a door still
hides them. Town edges do not wrap in Sightlines. The legacy mode keeps its
original behavior and is restored exactly when the option is turned off.

Tests cover straight and oblique halls, long halls/NPCs beyond the old window,
doors, L-shaped corners, opaque faces, hidden explored rooms, non-wrapping
edges, default migration, preset preservation and restoration of Classic.
Use tools/preview-town-sight.html for a save-free comparison, including a
long hall, closed-door toggle and a hidden wing revealed at the junction.
