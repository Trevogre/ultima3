Solid door leaves in the sprite-based 3D view

DoorCards now uses a tile-wide, one-tile-high box with 0.09-tile thickness.
Both broad faces retain the selected set's fitted door artwork. A narrow strip
of the same painting textures the edges, top and bottom; edges are shaded slightly
for readability. UVs keep the front/back and vertical grain upright in Z-up space.
Classic and full-overhead presentation retain their original flat tiles.

Unlock still consumes a key and changes the map tile to floor exactly as before.
mapDoors reads the pristine resource to retain the identity of the removed leaf,
then compares the current tile (or an occupying monster's under-tile). The renderer
admits these records only where its filtered terrain is known, not Void.
No new save data or gameplay state is needed: existing unlocked save tiles work.

A visible closed-to-open transition swings the leaf 90 degrees around its side
hinge over half a second, with eased motion. The hinge and neighboring-wall
orientation are fixed in world space, independent of camera rotation. The leaf
remains at 90 degrees after unlocking. A previously open door first encountered
or loaded from a save starts open. Returning to a freshly loaded town clears the
presentation cache. Animation follows map pause/visibility and does not advance
the gameplay clock. Model-set geometry is unchanged by this sprite-door change.

Save-free visual review: /tools/preview-door-swing.html.
Shows a closed door and an already-open door, Unlock, Reset, Rotate and tile sets.
Tests: doorCards.test.ts, doorStates.test.ts and the existing unlock tests.
