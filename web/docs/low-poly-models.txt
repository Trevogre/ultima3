3D model sets

F7 enters Models using the remembered set, then cycles Low-poly / Van Gogh.
The selected name appears briefly. F6 still selects the independent sprite
set. Both selections are remembered. Classic and Depth remain available.
Menus: Pause/Settings > Visuals > 3D set, or 3D Settings > 3D set.

Low-poly retains faceted model scenery. Van Gogh is a fuller procedural
model set: rounded canopies and figures, detailed towers, battlements,
windows and entrances, and UV-mapped surfaces. It samples material patches
from the bundled generated Van Gogh atlas (foliage, cloth, stone, wood,
skin and gold). The original artwork is unchanged. Paint loading is lazy,
works with the offline asset cache, and falls back to vertex colours.

Both sets animate independently of sprite ticks. Humanoids use articulated
joint groups: shoulders/elbows, hips/knees, torso and head. Continuous poses
include breathing/look-around idle, interpolated walking with a gait, weapon
attacks, casting gestures and hit recoil. Optional GameIO action cues feed
attacks/casts; normal visible hit data feeds recoil. Poisoned figures keep
their green tint. Flags flutter, vegetation sways, water moves, waterborne
props bob, and whirlpools rotate. Doors and other static props stay fixed.

The animation clock stops during menus/pauses and does not fast-forward on
resume. Movement uses stable entity IDs and handles map wrapping; teleports
snap rather than walking across the map. Camera rotation cannot turn the
models. Visibility-filtered inputs are shared with the other renderers,
and hidden rigs are immediately removed. Gameplay state is not changed.

Extension points: modelSets.ts registers stable saved IDs and menu metadata;
modelAssetSets.ts supplies scenery and actor factories. AnimatedModel's root,
pose, tint and disposal contract can also wrap a future skinned GLB with an
AnimationMixer. This increment uses jointed meshes, not skinned GLB assets.
No provider service, paid generation call or new download is required.

These are procedural sets, not a finished individually sculpted model for
every semantic tile. Some creatures still share family/fallback shapes.
Exact equipment models, death animations, richer creature rigs and bespoke
sculpted assets remain future work. First-person dungeons and menus retain
their established renderers. The Low-poly sign tops retain the selected
sprite glyphs; the Van Gogh model set has independent gold sign lettering.

Safe preview: /tools/preview-presentations.html, with set selection, F7,
walking, attack/cast/hit controls and pause. It owns synthetic terrain and
actors and never reads or writes game saves or display preferences.
