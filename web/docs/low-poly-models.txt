Low-poly model preview

Settings > Visuals > Presentation > Low-poly (preview), or toggle Style in
3D Settings. Classic and Depth remain available. The selected historical
or painterly tile set is preserved when switching model styles.

This initial pass uses locally authored procedural solid meshes, not a paid
asset provider. Model factories in modelAssets.ts cover terrain, trees,
brush, houses, castles, cave entrances, walls, shrines, chests, horses,
ships, portals, and directional humanoid / creature families. They use
faceted geometry with vertex colours, instancing, real depth testing and
directional light/shadow maps. They do not billboard toward the camera.
The next art stage can replace individual factories with reviewed GLBs.

Characters keep a presentation-only heading inferred from movement. Stable
party/monster IDs preserve it across redraws; wrapping map seams are handled
and large teleports do not turn a figure. Camera rotation never changes
heading. Party grid and follower layouts are supported. Actor selection
uses the ordinary visibility-filtered viewport; scenery uses the explored
landscape. No game state or exploration is written by this renderer.

This is a model-family prototype, not 96 individually sculpted, animated
assets. Several enemies intentionally share a family mesh; weapons/armour
are not yet exact equipment models. Functional letter tiles remain textures
on solid ground blocks. Menus and first-person dungeons keep their existing
renderers. Tile-specific card/filter/cube controls apply to the tile style;
camera, distant terrain, lowered water, sun shadows and day/night also work
with models. Real model shadows use the same world-space sun clock.

Safe visual fixture: /tools/preview-presentations.html. It owns synthetic
terrain and actors and never reads or writes saves or display preferences.
