This game bundles a small derived subset, not the full sample library.
Recordings were downmixed to mono, low-pass filtered and resampled to
22050 Hz PCM16, trimmed, peak-normalized, and given sustain crossfades
or harp release fades. Exact source paths and source/output SHA-256
hashes are recorded in manifest.json. No new compositions replace the
existing game music: the original note events drive a register-based
arrangement of cello, violin section, flute and harp.

The instruments use different source octave naming conventions. Manifest
root pitches were checked against the recordings, rather than inferred
uniformly from file names. The original game music has its existing
provenance and is not relicensed by this sample-bank license.
